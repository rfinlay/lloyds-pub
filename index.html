A West El Paso institution

Some bars chase trends. Lloyd's just keeps the drinks cold, the tab cheap, and the door open, the way it has for over 45 years.

No pretense, no dress code, no nonsense. Just the friendliest bartenders in town, a crowd that feels like family, and the kind of easy night out they don't really make anymore. Whether you're a regular or it's your first time, you're a local the second you walk in.
